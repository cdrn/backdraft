cross-venue realizable spread
The same asset trades on centralized exchanges and on-chain pools at slightly different prices. This watches all of them and asks, for every buy-here-sell-there pair of venues: after the exchange fee, gas, pool fee, book impact and rebalance, is there anything left — and does it survive landing one tick late? dex→dex on one chain is atomic (one transaction, no inventory); the other kinds carry inventory between venues. bps = hundredths of a percent.
